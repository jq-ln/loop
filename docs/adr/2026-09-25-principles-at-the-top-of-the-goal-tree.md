# The top of the goal tree is a set of principles, and there is no single root

Goal: G1. It ruled out a structure whose only warning that work had drifted from what it was for
could never fire, and setup that asks for authoring before the app does anything. G1 was not edited
after this work.
Affected paths: nothing

The sketch had given every goal a parent, defaulting to one root that could never be satisfied, so
that filing never blocked on deciding where a thing hung. That default was the trade given up here.

Principles bought a predicate that can fail: whether a principle is being served. It needed no new
machinery, being a roll-up over each principle's subtree of the dormancy and stall signals the sketch
already had. Under a single root that roll-up covered the whole app and said nothing. Under several
principles it said which stated value had no live work beneath it, and no other entity could say
that. The root was deleted rather than kept above the principles, because keeping it would have put
the vacuous predicate back one level up.

Three rules protected the predicate, and each was chosen over a cheaper option. A definition could
cite a principle directly: requiring a goal between them would have made every principle served by
construction, and half of what the author meant to enter on day one was standing practice, not
goals. The citation edge stayed a tree, with a single parent and no default. A principle was created
as its own act, never inline from filing, since adding one to house a stray goal is how the signal
dies.

Friction was the cost, and G1 bounds it. The app shipped with no principles, anything filed without
one sat in an unassigned pile, and nothing at setup asked for authoring. A principle served only by
standing practice was left unflagged, because that flag would have been a nag.

It was adopted with falsifiers. How often principles were revised should decay over a year of use,
and a principle created on the same day as the first thing filed under it, twice, marked the top
level becoming a filing drawer. Collapsing back to a single root would be a migration, which is why
this was recorded when it was decided and not at port time.
